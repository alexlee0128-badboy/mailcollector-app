隱私權政策 Privacy Policy

最後更新：2026-10-24

  • MailCollector 只存取開發者本人的 Gmail 帳號，不存取其他人的資料。
  • 用途：統計信件數量、為信件加標籤、在本人確認後把信件移到垃圾桶、找出與本人追蹤商品相關的優惠信。
  • 資料在本人的電腦上處理，不出售。例外：與追蹤商品相關的優惠信，其文字會送給 Google Gemini AI 判讀優惠內容。統計結果只發到本人的私人通知頻道。
  • 本工具不會寄出任何信件，也不會永久刪除信件。
  • 可隨時在 Google 帳戶的「第三方應用程式與服務」頁面撤銷存取權。
  • MailCollector accesses only the developer's own Gmail account.
  • Purpose: count mail, add labels, move mail to Trash after the owner confirms, and find offers related to items the owner tracks.
  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer and is never sold. Exception: the text of offer emails related to tracked items is sent to Google Gemini AI to read the offer. Results go only to the owner's private notification channel.
  • The tool never sends email and never permanently deletes email.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account "Third-party apps & services" page.
